## Responsibility of Society

Society, that is all of us, cannot rely on the social defenses of the
individual to keep them safe. We need to collectively develop and implement
social defenses to protect ourselves and future generations from the negative
impacts of abuse and hardship. By sharing our experiences and insights, we can
empower each other to create a safer and juster society.

But until that process is done, we need to take responsibility for the
environment people find themselves in. Physical aspects of environments are
taken care of in most cases (Harlingen was a disaster), but in the workspace
not enough counter measures are taken to guarantee psychosocial safety and
well-being. In part due to the fact that employees don't recognize patterns
nor know how to act upon encountering them, including managers. An

## What Social Defenses are needed

Martial arts make inventories of attacks and the defense techniques that one
martial art consists of. Then the techniques are linked to attacks and those
combinations are trained. How about social defenses? Let us make inventories
of attacks and effective defenses, then make combinations and possible
training exercises to master these techniques and combinations. Let us use
requirements engineering and the methodology introduced by Bertrand Meyer
with his book Handbook of Requirements and Business Analysis. The
requirements are written down but after that we keep reflecting and letting
them evolve.
