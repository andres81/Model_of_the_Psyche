## Introduction

An [article](https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/artikel/5649388/familierecht-advocaten-bedreiging-stalking)
by Rtl Nieuws infuriated me: A mother, an attorney, trying to do her job,
getting threats and being attacked, even visited at her domicile by an asshole
with EQ shoe size, and getting away with it with merely a "stop gesprek" by the
police. "Don't do it again! Naughty little boy!". If you don't pay your parking
tickets, it can escalate to a 10k+ amount you need to pay (the public servants
deserve their team building events, I guess... and that needs to be paid
somehow!). But if someone attacks an attorney, part of our legal system and
therefore our democracy, nothing in comparison if it comes to punishment! That
asshole should have been locked up for at least a year. Look at
what [they do in France](https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/buitenland/artikel/5650411/franse-vader-bedreigen-lerares-zoontje-basisschool-le-fauga-bij),
there they dó understand how to deal with weaker-minded  
individuals that need training and reprogramming. Thát is a statement by our
country, our nation. Protect our professionals, women, especially mothers. Where
was it department of Justice? Well? Time for action!
