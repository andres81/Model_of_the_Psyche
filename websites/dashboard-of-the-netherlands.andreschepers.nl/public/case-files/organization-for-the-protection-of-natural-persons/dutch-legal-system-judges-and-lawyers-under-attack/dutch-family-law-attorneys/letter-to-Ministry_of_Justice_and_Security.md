Ministry of Justice and Security, minister of Justice and Security van Weel,

I write you with regard to the research done by Investico and RTL Nieuws.

https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/artikel/5649388/familierecht-advocaten-bedreiging-stalking -
10 September 2026

What the hell of a reaction was:

*"Het mag niet zo zijn dat mensen die juridische hulp nodig hebben, geen
advocaat meer kunnen vinden. Juist in het familierecht, waar conflicten diep
kunnen ingrijpen in het leven van ouders en kinderen."*

Laissez-faire and disrespectful to the victim: So also, the attorneys, right?
RIGHT? I don't see anything regarding the deserved restorative and retributive
justice in your response to RTL Nieuws... Why? I delegate my responsibility of
keeping people safe to you, and then this is your response? "Go to the police,
they will perform a 'Stop' conversation...". Get to work!

I want to urge you to step up to the plate the coming weeks and get to work with
your ministry and the House of Representatives. What are we paying you for? The
moral system you represent? Your department failed already miserable regarding
oversight over the NS BOAs, together with the ILT, I want actions, now!
Retributive and restorative justice guaranteed or expect a request for an audit
of your ministry and the democracy in general in The Netherlands. With its
barbeque culture and laissez-faire cultural attitude regarding justice for
others until they want it for themselves.

Minister van Weel, the next time I see you on the telly, I want to see a
warrior, balls of steel, so heavy you need artificial support to move around. I
want to look up to our government, our ministers and the president, I want to
feel safe, like our king and queen make me feel safe in this country. Is that
too much to ask for? What kind of democracy are we living in here.

What I want: I want strict policies regarding protection of our protectors of
our democracy: The judges and attorneys. One-year unconditional imprisonment,
for example, for people that go to the homes of attorneys or judges. I fear
vigilantes becoming active otherwise that will start hunting these trashcans
sorry excuse of a human being that think they can intimidate citizens of this
country. Why was there no response like that in the above-mentioned article?
Still distracted by the memories of your summer holidays public servants? For
crying out loud...
