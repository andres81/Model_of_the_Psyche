Dear vFAS and Orde van advocaten,

I would like to ask what you have done or intent to do regarding legislation for
the protection and getting justice for lawyers that fall victim to human beings
that violate human rights of said lawyers. Do you plan on going or have you gone
to the House of Representatives to ask for new legislation if it is not yet in
place? Take for example meester Özge Özturk, what happened to her described in
this article:
https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/artikel/5649388/familierecht-advocaten-bedreiging-stalking.
Her human rights were violated but did she get retributive and restorative
justice? How about other attorneys? I as a citizen of The Netherlands wants this
guaranteed for them. But for that adequate legislation is required. You after
all, are professionals and experts regarding how the legal system works, and
what laws could help. You are citizens as well: We share the moral obligation to
protect each other.

In my opinion giving a reaction/response in a news article on the website of RTL
nieuws is simply not enough. Not as a citizen, nor as a professional
organization. Therefore the question what you have done, are doing and plan to
do to help the professionals in need of protection.

André Schepers
A concerned citizen of The Netherlands
