House of Representatives of The Netherlands

11 September 2026, The Netherlands

Dear members of the House of Representatives of The Netherlands,

This letter is regarding the research by Investico and RTL nieuws. Article
here: [Klemgereden, achtervolgd of gestalkt: familierechtadvocaten vrezen voor veiligheid](https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/artikel/5649388/familierecht-advocaten-bedreiging-stalking)

We, citizens of The Netherlands, delegate the responsibility to protect each
other and safeguard the democracy to moral systems; to you, the moral system
that the [House of Representatives](https://www.houseofrepresentatives.nl/) of
the Netherlands is, we delegate the responsibility of coming up with laws that
in part guarantee safety and security. But also, to deliver justice. All types:

* Distributive Justice
* Procedural Justice
* Retributive Justice
* Restorative Justice

Show me you're worth that delegation. Femicide is a huge and unacceptable
problem. What I see here is that trash human beings that commit forms of
femicide, proceed to expand their evil to the lawyers that try to protect the
victims: The women that are suffering. The lawyers that try to protect on behalf
of us, society. You want to tell me you don't care that women are protected? Or
that lawyers are attacked at home? And a mere "stop gesprek"
by the police is enough, according to you?

And then justice for the lawyers: Retributive and restorative justice. What
about meester Özge Özturk? Is she going to be given justice by society? Are we
sleeping politicians?

Looking at the list of atrocities, I believe enough is enough:

* bangalijsten
* Rapes in female prisons and other atrocities: No physical/psychosocial safety
  guaranteed
* Dutch childcare benefits scandal
* Eindhoven water cannon incident
* Tara...

The intervention should consist of much stricter laws regarding the freedom one
is given in The Netherlands. This means for individuals that visit the homes of
our attorneys, for example, one year in prison or something close to this, no
excuses. Just an example to be given. No slap on the wrist, but life-changing
measures. Better, more effective laws that shut down a situation to stop
individuals from intruding on the lives of the professionals that work to keep
our democracy working. But also provide retributive and restorative justice for
the victims: Attorneys, public servants and women in general. No more soft
approach, but ruthless interventions and making a statement. I hope that that
will prevent vigilantes from starting to hunt down these individuals that think
they live in a different context with different rules applying to them.

The only thing I can threaten you with is to go to the international community
to ask for an audit of the Dutch democracy to prevent a certain downfall.
Certain responsibilities were delegated, entrusted to you. I don't see the
respect given it deserves. In addition: Prevention of human rights violations.
For that, of course, we have
the [European Court of Human Rights](https://www.echr.coe.int/).

Looking at judges during the
[Dutch childcare benefits scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_childcare_benefits_scandal)
we can see that the sorry culture is back in full: You are "doomed to freedom".
So a judge could have resigned. But instead they became part of
[the banality of evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eichmann_in_Jerusalem#Banality_of_evil),
the engine of destruction that
the [CAF](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2023/07/05/eerste-deel-caf-onderzoek-openbaar)
had created; they let grow by becoming part of that dynamic. Judges... human
rights were violated... Really? (That chapter is not closed yet, and I will go
after áll the judges of The Netherlands for a justification why, at the very
least, they didn't urge for room to have power to interfere other than resign).

I am a citizen of The Netherlands. I represent the highest authority: The
institute citizenship. I merely delegate my responsibility until that is no
longer deserved by the moral systems in society I delegate to. That time has
come: I take back that responsibility that was always still mine and will get to
work to protect our democracy and the wellbeing of people in and of the
Netherlands, because politicians and public servants have failed blatantly.

André Schepers
