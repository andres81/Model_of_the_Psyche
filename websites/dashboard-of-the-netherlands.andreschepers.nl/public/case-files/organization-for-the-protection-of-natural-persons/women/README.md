## Training pipeline for women 18-24 years old

Would it not be a beautiful gift of society if young women received a training
pipeline that would teach them what they wouldn't learn by living life, even
after they lived a life far in their 50's, 60's or even 70's and 80's? Not only
learning what can be taught with modern-day (social) sciences, but also the work
that can be done during going through the pipeline?

The elements of the pipeline are at the very least:

* Social intelligence (Karl Albrecht / Daniel Goleman)
* Emotional Intelligence (Empathy is the key to success)
* Healing childhood trauma
* Healing emotional / psychological trauma
* Fixing internal coping mechanisms / external engagement strategies disbalance
* Cognitive data buffers: Overwriting implicit attitudes, making explicit ones /
  being aware of implicit ones and the resulting behaviors they cause. Being
  more resilient to the attempts of manipulation, and cognitive reprogramming. (
  "Games people play" is part of this intervention)
* Shadow work
* Inner child healing / mastery
* Conversation techniques mastery
* Mastering your body:
    * Fitness
    * Health
    * Eastern martial arts
    * Meditation
    * Sex education
* Spirituality
* Mindfulness
* Participating in society and building a career
* Love

      love until after the age of 35–40?
