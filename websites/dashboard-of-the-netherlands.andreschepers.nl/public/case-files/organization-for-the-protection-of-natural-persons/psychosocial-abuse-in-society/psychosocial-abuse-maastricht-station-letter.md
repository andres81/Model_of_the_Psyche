Subject: Psychosocial abuse Maastricht station

Arriva Nederland

At the Maastricht train station, 18 August 2026, there was a man walking in the
opposite direction of me on the bridge over the train rails of the Maastricht
train station. When he was two meters in front of me, it seems he yelled at an
Arriva colleague, if I remember correctly, something along the lines of:
["Hey, heb je de neus nog gezien? Wat een loser...".]([https://translate.google.com/?sl=nl&tl=en&text=Hey%2C%20heb%20je%20the%20neus%20nog%20gezien%3F%20Wat%20een%20loser...&op=translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=nl&tl=en&text=Hey%2C%20heb%20je%20de%20neus%20nog%20gezien%3F%20Wat%20een%20loser...&op=translate))

What I seem to remember, they were employees of the Arriva. But even if not, let
it then be a reminder to everyone involved, everyone present in Dutch society,
that psychosocial abusive behavior like the one described above, is not
acceptable. Not behind closed doors, not in public. I, as a citizen, have a
civil duty to protect the state and other people with my life, and I will. That
includes protection against psychosocial abusive behavior. Even if the behavior
is intended as a joke among colleagues. The climate of the train station by this
remark at that moment deteriorated. Other people there might have felt
threatened and unsafe.

In the future I will respond to this behavior by addressing the behavior
immediately with the person who behaved like this. It should be regarded as a
violation of the code of conduct of society, but also of the train station
Maastricht.
