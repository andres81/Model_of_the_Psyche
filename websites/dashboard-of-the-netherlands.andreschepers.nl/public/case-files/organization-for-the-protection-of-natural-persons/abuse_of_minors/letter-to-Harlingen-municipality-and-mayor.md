Dear mayor Sjerps and aldermen,

I write to you with regard to the sailing accident that killed Tara when being
present on a sailing boat that was part of the Brown Fleet, that was known to be
dangerous and potentially lethal. This was known due to research. Sources are
the media as you should know and should have known before this accident.

I wish to ask you through this letter, a full explanation of your actions, mayor
Sjerps, and that of the municipality, so also the citizens of your town. In my
honest opinion, this accident could have been prevented, and you, mayor Sjerps,
should have taken political responsibility in the form of resigning.

To explain where my reasoning comes from, the following excerpt from this
article to begin with:

https://www.omropfryslan.nl/nl/nieuws/881132/risico-op-houtrot-in-scheepsmasten-van-de-bruine-vloot-nog-te-groot -
24 april 2019

__*Te weinig met aanbevelingen gedaan*__

*De Onderzoeksraad deed aan de hand van een rapport diverse aanbevelingen die de
veiligheid aan boord moesten verbeteren. De raad concludeerde dat vakkennis over
belangrijke scheepscomponenten zoals masten nog onvoldoende gewaarborgd is. Het
onderzoek is onverbiddelijk: het ongeluk in Harlingen was geen incident dat op
zichzelf stond. Ernstige houtrot komt vaker voor in houten masten van de bruine
vloot. De aanbevelingen in het rapport zijn wel deels opgepakt, zo heeft de
Vereniging voor Beroepschartervaart een platform opgericht voor de veiligheid
aan boord. Toch geeft de Onderzoeksraad aan dat de betrokken partijen nog veel
meer verantwoordelijkheid moeten nemen om hun plannen ook daadwerkelijk uit te
voeren.*

__*Onderzoeksraad: eigenaren ook verantwoordelijk*__

*De huidige wijze van inspectie biedt dus geen garantie dat masten tot het einde
van de geldigheidsduur in de keuringscertificaten veilig blijven. De
Onderzoeksraad voor Veiligheid ziet graag dat er bij inspecties röntgenscans
worden gebruikt om houtrot op te speuren. De raad benadrukt wel dat exploitanten
en beheerders van schepen ook zelf verantwoordelijkheid dragen voor de
veiligheid en niet alleen moeten wijzen op de wettelijk verplichte keuringen.*

After the accident with Tara, apparently people woke up:

https://www.omropfryslan.nl/nl/nieuws/1168115/bruine-vloot-doet-aan-preventieve-ruiming-van-oude-masten -
13 september 2022

*Bruine vloot doet aan 'preventieve ruiming' van oude masten*

*De burgemeester van Harlingen, Ina Sjerps, sprak zich vlak na het ongeluk fel
uit. Volgens haar kon er van toeval geen sprake meer zijn na twee incidenten
kort na elkaar. Blaauw wil daar niet aan. "Je kunt vandaag ook met de auto voor
de apk en morgen de motor kapot hebben. Dat je zoiets zegt, slaat nergens op.
Laat ik eerlijk zijn: twee keer in één jaar is niet mooi, maar het blijft een
incident op zichzelf."*

The comparison of this accident with a car breaking down is interesting: An APK
is done to check if it is safe to allow the car on the road again. Yes, it can
break down, but that is not the reason an APK is done:

__*Bij de apk let de keurmeester onder andere op de volgende onderdelen:*__

*verkeersveiligheid (remmen, wielophanging, schokdempers, banden,
stuurinrichting, verlichting en carrosserie);*

Source wiki: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algemene_periodieke_keuring

These checks were also done with the Brown Fleet ships, and it was clear there
was a significant risk the masts would break due to undetected wood rot, as we
can read in the article above:

*Het risico op houtrot in scheepsmasten van de bruine vloot is nog altijd te
groot. Dat stelt de Onderzoeksraad voor Veiligheid naar aanleiding van enkele
ongelukken met verrotte houten masten, waaronder het dodelijke incident in
Harlingen in 2016. In maart 2019 kwam er in Zaandam nog eens een persoon om het
leven door een mastbreuk.*

This brings us to you mayor Sjerps and the municipality, including the citizens
of Harlingen. What responsibility did you take? If you know as a mayor of any
municipality of The Netherlands, that there is a high-risk situation in which
people might get harmed or killed, you have a duty of care to do everything in
your power to prevent anything from happening. At the very least, you could have
given very negative advice to anyone wishing to go on these ships to do so. My
question is then: What díd you do?

If you didn't know, how did that happen? How could you be the mayor without
being aware of the dangers? Your predecessor should have known or not? When you
took over, did your predecessor, mayor Sluiter, inform you of the dangers?

In my opinion, you should have known. If not, you should resign, in my opinion,
due to incompetence. If you did, you acted in bad faith, in my opinion, choosing
to not shut it down or do anything else if you didn't do anything. Therefore,
also this letter: I wish to know what the situation was, and currently is:
What are the policies to prevent this from ever happening again? Have you spoken
to other municipalities? Have you spoken with the De Vereniging van Nederlandse
Gemeenten (VNG)? Have they discussed this subject internally to take action when
action should be taken? They will also get this letter and the question of what
went wrong here. As also the high school Dalton Den Haag.

One final note: Our legal system doesn't allow getting retributive and
restorative justice very easily. Unlike the United States legal system, citizens
are not able to sue to get justice in all cases. The "polderen" model is active:
"At the end of the day we all learned, and we moved on..." No! If you, for
example, visit the homes of attorneys with the only consequence being a warning
from the police in the form of a "Stop!" gesprek, you did not serve as a society
retributive nor restorative justice to the attorney that is scared shitless now
at home! The banality of evil that Dutch citizens are part of... In this case,
if you are guilty of negligence, the parents and loved ones of Tara, deserve
justice. I am going for that, because of the simple reason I believe you were
grossly negligent together with the whole municipality and citizens of
Harlingen. The situation was not safe, clear facts. Only, when it is proven that
the situation is safe, one should allow the situation, the ships, to be
accessible again. You, a mayor in this country, are responsible for the safety
of situations in Harlingen. You might say the ILT was in part responsible, but
we had L'Être et le Néant, Jean-Paul Sartre and Simone de Beauvoir:

*"I want to be in New York. However, I was born in a little village in France,
that is the only time you don't decide place, and therefore you are not
responsible for being there, but after that, you decide the place you are in and
therefore the situation you have entered. That makes you in part responsible for
what happens in those situations."*

You stepped into the situation that is you fulfilling the role of mayor of
Harlingen. The moment you started as a mayor, you are responsible for knowing
how unsafe situations are. Not only that, also acting upon unsafe environments
as we see with the infrastructure in The Netherlands: A bridge farts and it's
closed. Zero risk!

After the accident, you were critical, you tried to be supportive, all actions
that are part of your role, forgetting the part of your role fulfillment that is
protecting people from entering dangerous situations in the first place. You can
imagine how infuriating your behavior is to me:

https://www.omropfryslan.nl/nl/nieuws/1167455/burgemeester-van-harlingen-wil-in-gesprek-met-bruine-vloot-ongeluk-met-charterschip-geen-incident-meer -
1 September 2022

*Burgemeester van Harlingen wil in gesprek met bruine vloot: ongeluk met
charterschip geen incident meer*

*"We kunnen niet meer zeggen dat dit een incident was." Dat zegt burgemeester
Ina Sjerps van Harlingen een dag na een dodelijk ongeluk met het charterschip
Risico. "We houden van onze bruine vloot, maar het moet wel veilig."*

For you, apparently safety is a "but it needs to be safe" aspect of reality. For
me it comes first: "It needs to be safe, but we love our fleet as well... so we
do all we can to make it possible for the ships to go out and set sail after we
have guaranteed safety for the full 100%." For you, it seems more a thing of:
"We love our Brown Fleet so they sail, but we need to make it safe, and we will
work very hard on this of course! After the calf has drowned..." No!

Of course, the Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management will hear from me
too: The ILT knows me, I know them, I'm coming this time with full destructive
force after them. The safety in The Netherlands is NOT guaranteed right now
especially not for our children.
