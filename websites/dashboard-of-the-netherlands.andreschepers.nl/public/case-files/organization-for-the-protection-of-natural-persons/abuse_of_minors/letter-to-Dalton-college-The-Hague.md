14 September 2026, The Netherlands

Dalton college The Hague,

I write you in regard to the incident with Tara, who died on a sailing boat of
the bruine vloot in Harlingen, August 2022. I have not read anything in the news
regarding measures taken to prevent this from ever happening again. Nor about
the retributive and restorative justice for the parents, family and loved ones
of Tara, in part coming from consequences for the municipality of Harlingen and
your highschool. In a separate letter to all "samenwerkingsverbanden vo" in The
Netherlands, I will give a strong warning with instructions that if it goes
wrong again, without having followed the instructions I give, nor what could
have been done at the very least to prevent accidents, I will drag every person
responsible in my view in front of the Human Rights committee: The citizens of
the international community (yes... not the European Court that in my opinion
has no effective role to play anymore). No questions asked, skipping the Dutch
legal system. The reason for this is that you as a highschool are a moral system
I delegate my responsibility to, to keep other citizens safe. After the warning
and instruction has been given by me to highschools and the government of The
Netherlands, and therefore to all citizens of this nation, breach of this
contract by disregarding the moral duty to do everything one can, is a reason to
go international with my escalation. By you failing to protect or at the very
least do everything in your power to prevent deadly accidents from happening, I
as a citizen and all citizens in The Netherlands have failed.

Questions:

__What policies changed with regard to safeguarding safety of students?__

__What was the result of internal research regarding not being aware of the
safety risks?__ Were they known or not, why not if not? If known, why was the
risk taken anyway? Did you not know the outcome of the Onderzoeksraad voor
veiligheid:

26 juli 2017

*Het is voor passagiers niet veilig op commerciële traditionele zeilschepen. Dat
concludeert de Onderzoeksraad voor Veiligheid (OVV).*

https://www.omropfryslan.nl/nl/nieuws/881132/risico-op-houtrot-in-scheepsmasten-van-de-bruine-vloot-nog-te-groot -
24 april 2019

*Het risico op houtrot in scheepsmasten van de bruine vloot is nog altijd te
groot. Dat stelt de Onderzoeksraad voor Veiligheid naar aanleiding van enkele
ongelukken met verrotte houten masten, waaronder het dodelijke incident in
Harlingen in 2016. In maart 2019 kwam er in Zaandam nog eens een persoon om het
leven door een mastbreuk.*

https://www.hartvannederland.nl/algemeen-nieuws/artikelen/school-van-meisje-12-dat-door-giek-omkwam-geschrokken-van-rapport -
27 sep 2023

*In 2017 bracht de Onderzoekraad ook al een rapport uit. Met de aanbevelingen
uit dat rapport zijn er wel initiatieven genomen om de vloot veiliger te maken,
maar het beoogde resultaat blijft volgens de Onderzoeksraad uit.*

If you knew, what made you think their could not be any wood rot anymore? I. e.,
the situation on board of a sailboat of the bruine vloot should have been safe.
In my opinion you had no reason whatsoever to assume it was safe to let students
on those boats!

__Is there a checklist now in place for parents before children are taken on
schooltrips?__

Did you have peer-group coaching ("intervisie") with high schools at the
national level? If yes, what was the outcome? If not, why not? What did you do
to help prevent this from happening again?

__Pointing fingers__

https://www.hartvannederland.nl/algemeen-nieuws/artikelen/school-van-meisje-12-dat-door-giek-omkwam-geschrokken-van-rapport -
27 sep 2023

*"De conclusies wijzen uit dat de aanbevelingen die voortkwamen uit een
onderzoeksrapport uit 2017 op veel fronten volledig genegeerd zijn," deelde de
school in een verklaring. "In deze moeilijke tijd heeft de school dringend
behoefte aan concrete antwoorden en een duidelijk stappenplan van zowel de
historische zeilschipbranche als het verantwoordelijke ministerie."*

You are pointing fingers to other institutions: Moral systems. But I don't see
in that news article that you share that you point fingers at yourself. Why is
that? Were you saying with your response that you felt being a victim? You were
not at all responsible? Please a full justification for your actions.

### Conclusion

Dalton college, being a high school, means you are a moral system. I, as a
citizen of this country, delegate my responsibility to you to keep people safe.
When that fails, the responsibility comes back to me. You failed. Therefore, I
write you to ask for answers to the questions above.

André Schepers
